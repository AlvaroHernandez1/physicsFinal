# physicsFinal

We are recreating a problem on conservation of angular momentum from the first semester. In said simulation, two skaters on frictionless ice move along opposite parallel paths, when they hit and grab onto a pole and rotate. 

We will be allowing the user to control the skaters respective masses and intial velocities, as well as the pole which will have significant moment of inertia. The user can also pick where the skaters collide with the pole. We are also considering adding friction to slow the system to a stop.

We will graph or display the kinetic energy of the system, their angular velocities, and their linear velocities.
